🗡️ Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom

Nintendo • 2023 • Action-Adventure

The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom is the sequel to Breath of the Wild. Link returns to explore a dramatically transformed Hyrule, with new sky islands above and mysterious depths below the surface.


The game introduces new abilities like Ultrahand, Fuse, Ascend, and Recall, allowing players to build vehicles, weapons, and contraptions to solve puzzles and traverse the world in creative ways.

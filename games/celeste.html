🏔️ Celeste

Maddy Makes Games • 2018 • Platformer

Celeste is a critically acclaimed precision platformer about climbing a mountain. You play as Madeline, a young woman determined to reach the summit of Celeste Mountain, facing both physical challenges and her inner demons.


With tight controls, brilliant level design, an assist mode for accessibility, and a deeply personal story about anxiety and self-acceptance, Celeste is widely regarded as one of the best platformers ever made.

🪲 Hollow Knight

Team Cherry • 2017 • Metroidvania

Hollow Knight is a 2D action-adventure game set in the vast, interconnected underground kingdom of Hallownest. Players control the Knight, a mysterious silent protagonist, as they explore twisting caverns, ancient cities, and deadly wastes.


With tight combat, gorgeous hand-drawn art, and an atmospheric soundtrack, Hollow Knight offers dozens of hours of exploration, challenging boss fights, and deep lore to uncover.

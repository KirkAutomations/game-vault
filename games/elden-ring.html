⚔️ Elden Ring

FromSoftware • 2022 • Action RPG • Open World

Elden Ring is an action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. Directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki with worldbuilding by George R. R. Martin, the game takes place in the Lands Between, a vast open world full of danger and discovery.


Players take on the role of a Tarnished, exploring a massive interconnected world filled with dungeons, bosses, and secrets. The game combines the challenging combat FromSoftware is known for with unprecedented freedom of exploration.

The Marketplace

“Man is a rope, tied between beast and superman — a rope over an abyss.”
Zarathustra speaks above the marketplace. The wind carries words past him — click one to speak it. The walker on the rope is your meter: a true word steadies him; a flattering word tips him toward the crowd. The crowd will show you what it heard.

Prologue · §6

The Fall

The walker lies broken. His eyes are open. Hold SPACE to keep your hand on his — he can only hear you while you stay close. Choose a gesture with the mouse. Watch his eyes when you hover; they tell what he will hear. The lantern flickers when he is failed. Three breaths. No more.
The tightrope walker is halfway across.
A jester has emerged from the tower.

The body grows cold.

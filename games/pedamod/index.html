Quick Reference

  • 26 bones, 33 joints, >100 ligaments
  • Windlass mechanism — plantar fascia tightens during toe dorsiflexion to stiffen the longitudinal arch
  • Truss model — calcaneus + metatarsal heads = struts; plantar fascia = tie rod
  • Rich proprioceptive field: ~104 mechanoreceptors per cm² in glabrous plantar skin

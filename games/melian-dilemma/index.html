How to Play

Campaign Mode: You are an advisor to Athens during the Peloponnesian War (431-404 BC). Navigate 8 historical scenarios by making strategic choices that reflect real IR Realism concepts.

  • Military Power — Your state's coercive capability and hard power projection.
  • Political Wisdom — Understanding of realist strategy and rational statecraft.
  • Reputation — How allies and rivals perceive your credibility and reliability.

Each decision teaches a core concept of IR Realism drawn from Thucydides' account. The "best" answer aligns with classical realist strategy — but every choice reveals something about international politics.

Quick Quiz: Test your knowledge of IR Realism concepts, key quotes, and Peloponnesian War history in a 10-question challenge.

Codex: An unlockable encyclopedia of IR Realism concepts. Play the campaign to discover all entries!

All quotes are drawn from Thucydides' History of the Peloponnesian War, the foundational text of the realist tradition in international relations theory.

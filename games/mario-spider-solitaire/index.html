🎮 How to Play / Cómo Jugar

🃏 Spider Solitaire Rules

  • Build columns of cards in descending order (K, Q, J, 10, 9... A)
  • Tap a card to select, then tap where to move it (or drag!)
  • Complete a K→A sequence to clear it! Do this 8 times to win 🏆
  • Tap the ? stock pile to deal 10 new cards (all columns must have cards)
  • Only face-up cards in descending order can move together

🧠 Spanish Challenges / Desafíos en Español

  • Every move requires answering a Spanish question! 🇪🇸
  • Correct ✅ = Move executes + earn a coin
  • Wrong ❌ = See the answer, move denied, lose a coin
  • Get 5 in a row = earn a ⭐ Power Star (free move!)
  • Words get harder as you play — you'll learn numbers, colors, animals, food, family, and common phrases!

🌟 Tips / Consejos

  • Check your 📖 Word Bank to review learned words
  • Master a word (3 correct) to earn a ⭐ on it!
  • Completing sequences reveals philosophy wisdom! 🏛️

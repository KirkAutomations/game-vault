🔥 Hades

Supergiant Games • 2020 • Roguelike

Hades is a roguelike dungeon crawler where you play as Zagreus, son of Hades, attempting to escape the Underworld. Each run is different, with randomized power-ups from the Olympian gods and an ever-evolving story.


The game is celebrated for its fast-paced combat, stunning art direction, incredible voice acting, and a narrative that brilliantly uses the roguelike structure — death isn't failure, it's progress.
